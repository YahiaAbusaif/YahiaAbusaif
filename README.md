# Yahia Abusaif

Hey. I love problem solving in general, and I love the structure of working a problem through. I practice competitive programming sometimes, but the field is not my type. The problems I like are the messy work ones, where the technical side and the business side are telling two different stories. I sit with a teammate on the technical side, and with the business on the other, until both are talking about the same thing. Data is usually how I get there. I liked it as a student, and the job is where it became useful: look until the business can see what the product is actually doing, then say what we should do. At Sahl that was one picture of the app instead of a pile of analytics tools, deep links and campaigns included. At Microsoft it was campaign priority. Less playful, same kind of problem.

I do that work in writing. A short note or a design, mainly so the person who joins later, or missed the meeting, can catch up without hunting someone down. I like the design to be challenged. Either it moves to a better state, or we can say why this version fits our case, and the people who only read it still learn. I would rather finish that on the page than in a pairing call, because a call keeps the reason in two heads. I mentor lightly. Enough that someone can move, not so much that they stop thinking. I also like a quiet pass first: draft it, understand it, then bring the outcome to other people.

I write, and I taught kids for six years at a charity. I would like to teach again. I am looking at a master's, and I think it should be AI. I have not closed that decision.

I am curious about data science, cybersecurity, and how open source projects are changing the way they build. I have not gone deep on all of it. What I already do, on a team or in a repo, is read the problem, write the decision down, and challenge the design until it is clear.

I like anime, manga, and games. I keep a collection of indoor plants and flowers: aglaonema, pothos, dracaena, calathea, and others. I love animals. I used to have rabbits, a hedgehog, and a hamster, and right now I have many types of birds.
